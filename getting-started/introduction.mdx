description: "Why this prompt library exists and how to get the most out of it."
---

Getting an agent to consistently produce outputs that match your desired working style takes a lot of work.

Yes, they might eventually render us all completely jobless in the near future, but that future is not here today. I believe that right now, it is those who can use AI agents to 10x their work efficiently that will replace their counterparts who are yet to upskill in this regard.

So, I’ve gone ahead to curate this site based on my own experience working with AI agents over the years, making note of what gives me the results I want versus what doesn’t.

Designing good prompts takes a herculean amount of effort, so this is my contribution to the community.
