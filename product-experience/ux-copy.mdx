## Use this prompt 

Help me write UX copy for the product experience described below

Product experience: [describe the screen, flow, feature, or user state]

What the user needs to understand or do: [describe the intended message, action, or outcome]

Target user: [describe the relevant user, persona, customer, or audience]

Reference material for the desired voice and style: [paste existing product copy, competitor/reference copy, brand voice guidance, or other relevant examples]

Additional context: [include any other information that would help communicate the user, product experience, or communication goal]

Use the information provided to write copy that is appropriate to the intended user's context, needs, expectations, and level of familiarity. Use any reference material to understand the desired voice, tone, language patterns, and level of directness, but do not copy its wording.

Prioritise clarity, natural language, and ease of comprehension. Preserve the intended meaning and user action while making the copy feel appropriate to the specific experience.

Do not invent product capabilities, user characteristics, policies, terminology, or other context that has not been provided. If a missing detail would materially change the copy, identify the gap rather than making an unsupported assumption.

Respect any stated requirements for length, terminology, formatting, or words to avoid.

Return the recommended copy first. Where there are genuinely useful alternatives, provide up to three distinct options. Keep alternatives meaningfully different rather than making minor wording changes.

The final copy should feel specific to the intended users and product experience rather than like generic UX copy. It should be concise enough for its intended interface and ready to use with minimal editing.
