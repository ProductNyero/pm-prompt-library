# Develop a Product Backlog

> Use this prompt to develop and groom product backlog based on data insights

## Use this prompt 

Develop a prioritised product backlog based on the customer and product information provided below

Product/company: [product or company]

Quarter: [quarter]

Reference materials: [customer surveys, support tickets, reviews, interviews, customer feedback, product analytics, or other relevant inputs]

Existing backlog / known issues: [attach or paste, if available]

Additional context: [any other relevant information]

Analyse the reference materials to identify the problems, pain points, friction, unmet needs, and other issues that should be represented in the backlog. Look across the inputs to identify recurring problems rather than treating each mention as an isolated item.

Prioritise the resulting backlog according to the frequency with which each problem or pain point appears across the available evidence. Use the strength and consistency of the signal to determine whether an item represents a high, moderate, or low level of pain. Do not treat frequency as the only relevant evidence when the available context indicates that the occurrences are materially different.

Do not invent customer problems, supporting evidence, or severity. Distinguish recurring signals from isolated feedback and consolidate multiple references to the same underlying problem into a single backlog item where appropriate.

Return the backlog as a Kanban board with three columns: High, Moderate, and Low. Each backlog item should clearly describe the action or work required to address the underlying problem.

For each item, include a concise justification for its priority based on the evidence provided.

The resulting board should make it immediately clear which problems have the strongest recurring signal and therefore warrant the greatest attention.
