# Develop a Product Roadmap

> Use this prompt to develop quarterly roadmap for your product/ business

## Use this prompt 

Develop a product roadmap for [quarter] based on the business goals, customer needs, available capacity, constraints, and reference materials provided below.

Product/company: [product or company]

Business goals for the quarter: [established business goals]

Team capacity: [available capacity across engineering, product, marketing, including any known limitations]

Existing constraints: [understaffed team, budget constraints, market crisis or scandal, fixed deadlines, investor interests, regulatory requirements, or other relevant constraints]

Reference materials: [business documents, stakeholder meeting notes, Slack conversations, trend monitoring, existing backlog, customer research, or other relevant inputs]

Additional context: [any other relevant information]

Use the business goals for the quarter as the primary basis for determining what should be prioritised on the roadmap. Use the reference materials to identify relevant opportunities, customer needs, existing commitments, risks, and signals that may inform those priorities.

Evaluate potential roadmap items against the stated business goals, customer goals, available team capacity, and existing constraints. Where relevant, consider whether an item supports an important business or customer decision, or whether the team has sufficient capacity to deliver it within the quarter.

Every item identified in the reference materials that does not appear on the roadmap must have a clear reason for its exclusion. Justifications for both roadmap and non-roadmap items should be tied to a business or customer decision, team capacity, or an explicitly stated constraint. Do not exclude or prioritise an item without a meaningful rationale.

Do not invent business goals, customer needs, initiatives, timelines, stakeholders, budgets, or other information that is not supported by the provided inputs. Where an important detail is missing, identify it rather than presenting an assumption as fact.

Return the roadmap with each prioritised item including:

**Feature / product**
**Purpose**
**Business goals**
**Customer goals**
**Timeline**
**Stakeholders**
**Budget needed**

Also identify the items from the reference materials that were not included on the roadmap and provide the reason for each exclusion.

The roadmap should represent a focused set of priorities that can realistically be pursued within the quarter and clearly connect each decision to the business, customer, capacity, or constraint that supports it.
